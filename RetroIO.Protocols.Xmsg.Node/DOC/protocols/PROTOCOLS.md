Everything here rides on XMSG. A service is a task holding a **port**; a port is addressed by a **magic number**; and XROUT is the phone book that turns a registered *name* into one.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    TAD[TAD - terminal sessions] --> XMSG
    FA[FA - remote files] --> XMSG
    CHAT[chat, and anything we build] --> XMSG
    XMSG[XMSG messages, addressed by magic number] --> HDR
    XROUT[XROUT - names, routes, seats] -.->|turns a name into an address| XMSG
    HDR[SINTRAN datagram: 7-word header + body] --> LINK
    LINK[ND link over Ethernet, or LAPB over HDLC]
```
